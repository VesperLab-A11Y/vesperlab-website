The Lab

Bilingual tools I build for my own work, published in the open, accessible, and reusable.

  • Vesper Toolkit

    A set of bookmarklets you drag into your bookmarks bar: an axe-core scan, link/image/form-label checks, a CSS selector, and new additions as they come.

  • Vesper Library

    A library of accessible interface components, tested with a screen reader, with the code and an explanation of what makes each one usable.

  • Vesper Compass and Sonar

    All 87 criteria translated and documented, freely available. The base every Lab tool builds on, growing with each audit.

Who I am

Five years as a paramedic, I studied sociology and the biases of the medical field from the break room, then moved into web design and development. Every hat I've worn traces back to the same thread: who goes unseen, how they got erased, and how to switch the light back on.

Vesper Lab grew out of those blind spots, and the wish to see clearly. I work from Montréal, in French and English.
