What I care about

  • Holding multiple points of view

    I notice barriers because I learned to notice them from several angles at once: care work, sociology, design, and code. Expanding what's possible starts with understanding what a system even is, why it's there, how it gets built, how it works, and how it fits into everything around it.

  • Making an impact

    I work for the public, with organizations that want to change things. Making the second group visible to the first already leaves a lasting mark toward a more accessible web.

  • Getting the message across

    I use my voice to make things clear: articles, workshops, plain-language explanations. I push for concrete action, grounded in the disability creation process (PPH), a social and environmental model of disability.

  • Thinking accessibility as plural

    I build free, accessible, open-source tools, held to the same standards as the sites I audit, so people can teach themselves web accessibility. Everything I make, projects, content, and audit reports alike, is meant to be accessible, practical, and usable with your eyes closed.
